Privacy and data

What stays in this browser

Shop settings, recent sessions, transcripts, khata entries and keys saved in Settings live in this browser’s site data. Other people using the same browser profile can access them.

To remove this data, clear KiranaVaani’s site data in your browser. This removes shop settings, khata entries and session history together. You can remove just the browser’s saved Sarvam key in Settings.

What goes to your voice provider

Browser speech recognition can send recordings to your browser provider. When Sarvam voice is active, KiranaVaani sends speech, order messages and replies to Sarvam for transcription, conversation and speech. Typed orders use the local catalogue when Sarvam is off.

UPI payments

The QR contains the payee UPI ID, shop name and bill amount. Your payment app handles the transfer. Check the payee and the amount there before you send money.

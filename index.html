How it works

From the shelf to the bill.

  1. 01

    Ask for an item

    Say “Aashirvaad atta 5 kg.” The counter listens like a call. You can interrupt if you need to change the order.

  2. 02

    Check what’s in stock

    If Basmati is out, the counter can offer Ponni rice. It adds the substitute when the customer says yes and records what was requested.

  3. 03

    Read the bill and scan to pay

    Say “bill” to hear the items and total. Say “pay” for a UPI QR. When the session ends, the receipt and transcript go to the dashboard.

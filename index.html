How it works

Three steps, no app, no typing.

01

Customer speaks

“Aashirvaad atta 5 kg irukka?” — in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, English, Malayalam or Marathi, native script or Tanglish. The agent listens like a phone call: it speaks, then listens again, and the customer can interrupt it mid-sentence.

02

Agent checks & suggests

Stock is checked on the shelf. If Basmati is out, the agent offers Ponni instead — “shall I?” — and a “yes” adds it. The running bill updates with every item, and every missed request is logged as a restock signal.

03

Scan the QR & pay

“Bill” reads the itemised bill out loud; “pay” shows a UPI QR for the exact total. The customer scans with GPay, PhonePe or Paytm. The session summary — items, total, signals — lands on the owner dashboard.

Features

A full shop counter, not a chatbot.

Voice-first sessions

Continuous turn-taking — the agent listens, thinks, speaks, then listens again. Barge-in: speak over the agent and it stops to hear you. Session timer, live transcript and a summary at the end.

Live stock check

Every order is checked against the shelf before it is confirmed. Partial stock is handled honestly — “only 50 kg is left, I added that much.”

Smart substitutes

Out of stock? The agent suggests the nearest same-category item in stock and waits for a “yes”. No silent swaps, ever.

UPI QR checkout

The bill total becomes a scannable UPI QR, generated on the device. Works with any UPI app; the shop's own VPA is set in Settings.

Restock signals

Every missed request is logged with the item, quantity and language — the shop sees exactly what customers asked for and could not get.

Owner dashboard

Today's orders, revenue, top sellers, low-stock alerts and full voice-session history — across three demo shops you can switch between.
